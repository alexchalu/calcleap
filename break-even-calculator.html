What is Break-Even Analysis?

Break-even analysis is a financial calculation that determines the point at which revenue equals expenses. This is the point where your business neither makes a profit nor incurs a loss.

Break-Even Point = Fixed Costs ÷ (Price - Variable Cost per Unit)

Fixed Costs: Costs that remain constant regardless of production volume (rent, salaries, insurance)

Variable Costs: Costs that change with production volume (materials, labor, shipping)

Price: Selling price per unit

Contribution Margin: The amount each unit contributes to covering fixed costs
